About Pakistan

A country of remarkable landscapes, diverse cultures, historic civilizations and national pride.

A Land of Pride and Possibilities

Pakistan is a country in South Asia that became independent on 14 August 1947. Its creation is closely associated with the Pakistan Movement and the leadership of Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah.

Islamabad is the capital of Pakistan, while Karachi is its largest city. The country has four provinces: Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan.

Pakistan is home to ancient archaeological sites, historic mosques, colourful bazaars, fertile plains, deserts, forests, rivers and spectacular mountains.

Its economy includes agriculture, manufacturing, technology, trade and services. Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad are important centres for education, business, history and modern development.

Our national motto: Unity, Faith and Discipline — values associated with Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah.

A Symbol of Our Nation

The National Flag of Pakistan

Our flag represents national identity, unity and the ideals on which our country was founded.

Our Green and White Flag

The national flag of Pakistan was designed by Syed Amir-uddin Kedwaii and adopted on 11 August 1947.

The green field represents the Muslim majority, while the white stripe represents religious minorities. The crescent is associated with progress, and the five-pointed star represents light and knowledge.

  • Green: Muslim majority
  • White: Religious minorities
  • Crescent: Progress
  • Star: Light and knowledge

Our Traditions

Culture of Pakistan

Pakistan's culture reflects a rich mix of regional languages, customs, art, food and celebrations.

👘

Traditional Clothing

Shalwar kameez is widely worn across Pakistan. Ajrak, Sindhi caps and regional embroidery express local identity.

🍲

Food and Cuisine

Biryani, nihari, karahi, chapli kebab, saag and regional breads are enjoyed throughout the country.

🎉

Festivals and Celebrations

Eid, Independence Day, weddings and regional festivals bring families and communities together.

🎵

Music and Literature

Folk music, qawwali, poetry and literature in Urdu and regional languages are important parts of our cultural heritage.

🏏

Sports and Recreation

Cricket is widely followed. Field hockey, football, squash and traditional games are also part of Pakistan's sporting life.

🧵

Arts and Handicrafts

Pottery, weaving, truck art, embroidery, woodwork and handmade textiles showcase generations of creativity.
